## What it does

Education works like a course platform. Admins build courses from sections and lessons, and staff add a course to a patient's portal. Patients see course cards with a cover image and their progress, and take one lesson at a time. The library starts empty, ready for the clinic's content.

## How it works

1. An admin opens **Education** and creates a course with a title, then adds sections (**New section**) and lessons (**New lesson**). Sections and lessons can be reordered.
2. A lesson is written in an editor with headings, bold, lists, quotes and links. It can hold a YouTube, Vimeo or Loom video that plays on the page, and PDF or image downloads (**Add a file**).
3. A lesson is a draft until it is published. The team can preview a course as a patient sees it.
4. Staff add a course to a patient's portal with **Add a course to their portal**.
5. The patient opens **Education**, sees their courses and progress, and completes lessons one at a time.

## How to try it

1. As an admin, open **Education** and create a course with one section and two lessons. Add a video link to one.
2. Preview the course.
3. Publish it, then add it to a test patient's portal.
4. As the patient, open **Education**, take a lesson and complete it. The progress moves.

**What you should see:** a draft lesson is never visible to the patient.
