## What it does

The screens an admin keeps up to date so the rest of the clinic can work: who is on the team, which blood panels and fees exist, which appointments can be booked online, and the records the CQC may ask for.

## How it works

- **Team and settings.** Invite people, resend or cancel an invitation, and see every invitation. A team member who leaves is switched off in the database by marking their staff profile inactive: access to every clinic screen ends at once, their history stays, and nothing is deleted. There is no screen for it yet; we do it on request.
- **Panels and fees.** The list of blood panels (code, name, price, fasting, tubes, who each is for, and the preparation shown to the patient) and the two route fees. An admin can load a whole list from a spreadsheet: it is checked and shown first, and nothing is deleted.
- **Booking settings.** Each clinician's weekly hours, and which appointment types are offered online.
- **Results sign-offs.** Every released report, with the doctor who signed it off, who ordered the tests, whether action was needed, and the action. It is for the team only, and nobody can change it afterwards.
- **Feedback.** The CQC download of positive feedback and the full log with follow-ups.
- **Activity.** Who opened or changed which patient's record, and when. Every invitation, release, refund and download is here. The log is append-only.
- **Refunds.** Only an admin refunds a blood test order or the fee of a declined repeat request, always in full.

## How to try it

1. Open **Panels and fees**, change a panel's price, and see it appear on the next order.
2. Load a small spreadsheet of panels, read the preview, then confirm.
3. Open **Results sign-offs** after a result has been released.
4. Open **Activity** and find the steps you took.

**What you should see:** only admins reach Panels and fees, Results sign-offs and Activity. A PA does not.
