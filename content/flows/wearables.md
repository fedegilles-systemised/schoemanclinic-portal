## What it does

A patient can link an Oura or WHOOP account so that one figure per day arrives on their record: sleep, sleep score, readiness or recovery, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, steps, active minutes, blood oxygen, temperature change and VO2 max, as far as the provider offers them. The clinic team reads the figures next to readings typed in by hand.

It needs the patient's consent, and an approved developer app for each provider in the clinic's name (see the Roadmap). Garmin and Withings are not built.

## How it works

1. On **Wearables**, the patient reads what is shared and agrees to "Connecting my wearables". The consent is recorded with the version of its wording.
2. They choose **Connect Oura** or **Connect WHOOP**, sign in at the provider and allow access to daily figures only. Clinic Hub never sees their provider password.
3. Once a day Clinic Hub reads each linked account. The first read happens at once and goes back 30 days.
4. Figures read this way show the device's name. A patient cannot remove one: they disconnect instead.
5. **Disconnect** stops the reading and removes the stored access. Withdrawing the consent does the same for every wearable at the next daily run. Figures already saved stay on the record.
6. If a provider stops accepting the link, the screen asks the patient to connect again.
7. The stored access codes are encrypted and never shown to anyone.

## How to try it

You need your own Oura or WHOOP account, and the provider's approval.

1. As a test patient, open **Wearables** and give the consent.
2. Press **Connect Oura** (or **Connect WHOOP**) and sign in at the provider.
3. Wait for the first read, then open the patient's record as a doctor to see the figures.
4. Press **Disconnect** and check that new figures stop arriving.

Without an account you can still type a reading by hand on the same screen.
