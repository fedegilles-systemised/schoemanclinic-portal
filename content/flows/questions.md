## What it does

A patient, or a guardian who can act, writes to the clinic from the portal. The question and every reply are kept on the patient's record, so the team no longer has to copy them from a mailbox. The page says it is not for emergencies and points to 999, A&E and NHS 111.

## How it works

1. The patient opens **Ask the clinic**, writes a subject and a message, and marks it routine or important.
2. The clinic inbox is emailed that a question is waiting, with no details. The team sees it under **Questions** in the clinic menu, and on the patient's record.
3. A team member who can open that patient replies. The patient is told by email, by WhatsApp where it is on, and in the portal bell, without the words of the reply.
4. The patient can write back. The team closes the conversation when nothing more is needed.
5. The question is also kept as a permanent copy in the patient's forms.

## How to try it

1. As a test patient, open **Ask the clinic** and send a question.
2. As a PA or doctor, open **Questions**, open the question and reply.
3. As the patient, check the email and the bell. The notice says there is a reply, never what it says.
4. Reply from the patient's side, then close the conversation as the team.

**What you should see:** a doctor outside the patient's care team cannot see the question.
