## What it does

After an appointment the patient is asked how it went. The answer is rated green, amber or red, followed up by the team and kept on the patient's record. Positive feedback can also be kept, without names, as evidence for the CQC.

| Rating | Score | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Green | 9 or 10 | The patient is offered the clinic's Google review link |
| Amber | 7 or 8 | Admins and PAs are told; follow-up within two working days |
| Red | 0 to 6, or asking to be contacted | Admins, PAs and the patient's doctors are told; follow-up the same working day |

## How it works

1. One to three days after an appointment, a daily job asks for feedback. It counts a visit marked completed, or one still booked or confirmed once it has ended. Cancelled visits and no-shows are left out. A patient is asked at most once every 90 days.
2. The survey goes by email, and by WhatsApp if the patient chose it. One reminder goes three days later. A survey closes after 30 days.
3. In the portal the patient answers: how likely they are to recommend the clinic (0 to 10), what went well, what could be better, whether they want to be contacted, and whether their comments may be used without their name.
4. A green answer shows the Google review link. Others are thanked and told someone will be in touch.
5. On the **Feedback** screen the team sees what is waiting, contacts the patient and records what was done and the outcome. A recorded follow-up cannot be changed.
6. For the CQC, the screen downloads the positive feedback the patient agreed may be used, and a full log with the follow-ups. A person checks that a comment names nobody and says nothing about health before it goes in the download. Every download is logged.

Working days skip weekends and bank holidays in England and Wales. Clinic emails never contain a name or a comment.

## How to try it

The survey goes out from a daily job one to three days after a visit, so the quickest way to see it is for us to send one. Tell us which test patient to use.

1. Make sure the test patient has a completed appointment.
2. We trigger the job. The patient receives the survey.
3. Answer it as the patient with a score of 6. A red follow-up appears on **Feedback** for the team.
4. Record a follow-up on it.
5. Answer a second survey with a 10 to see the review link.

**What you should see:** the review link appears only after a green answer, and only if the clinic's Google review link has been set.
