## What it does

Nobody signs up on their own. A person is invited, accepts the invitation, chooses a password and lands on the right home screen: the patient portal for patients and guardians, the clinic portal for the team.

- **Who invites.** A PA invites patients and guardians. A doctor invites their own patients. An admin invites anyone: patients, guardians, doctors, nurses, health coaches, PAs and admins.
- **What they get.** Patients and guardians see the portal. Staff see the clinic portal and every patient. The care team says who looks after each patient.
- **Every invitation is logged** under the sender's name, with the access it grants.

## How it works

1. The sender opens **Team and settings** and chooses the kind of invitation. For a patient they pick a patient already on the records, or add a new one with a name and date of birth.
2. For a patient, the address should match the one on the record. If it does not, the sender sees a warning and has to confirm.
3. The person gets an email with a one-time link, valid for 3 days. The email says nothing clinical and never names the patient.
4. They open the link. A patient or guardian is asked for a date of birth: their own, or the patient's for a guardian. After five wrong dates the link is cancelled and the sender is told.
5. They enter their name and choose a password. Someone who already has an account signs in and presses one button to add the new access to it, for example a doctor who is also a patient.
6. Whenever a doctor or an admin is invited, the other admins and Dr Gina Schoeman are told. When a guardian who can act is invited, the other PAs are told.
7. A patient added from 19 November 2026 goes on to the registration form before the portal (see the registration guide).

**Signing in.** Email and password. A suspended account, or one with no role, is told it does not have access. Staff sessions end after 12 hours or 30 minutes idle. Patient sessions end after 7 days or 2 hours idle. **Forgot password** sends a link; the reply is the same whether or not the address has an account.

**Profile.** Everyone can change their photo, name, phone, notification choices and password from the account menu. Changing the sign-in email asks for the password and sends a link to the new address; the old address is told.

## How to try it

Use a fictional patient and an email address you can open.

1. Sign in as a PA or admin and open **Team and settings**.
2. Invite a new patient: a made-up name, a date of birth and your own test address.
3. Open the email and follow the link. Enter the date of birth you gave, then choose a password.
4. You land on the registration form. Continue with the registration guide, or stop here.
5. Sign out and use **Forgot password** on the sign-in page to see the reset email.
6. Try a wrong date of birth five times on a second invitation to see the link close.

**What you should see:** the email names nothing about the patient, the link works once, and the invitation appears in the activity log (admins open **Activity**).
