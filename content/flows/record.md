## What it does

A patient's record is where the team keeps everything about one patient. Every doctor, nurse, health coach, PA and admin can open any patient's record; the care team says who looks after the patient. Every opening and every change is written to a log nobody can edit.

On the record:

- **Notes.** Clinical notes, written here and final once signed. A signed note is locked: nobody edits or corrects it.
- **Allergies, diagnoses and medicines.** Doctors record diagnoses. Nurses and doctors record allergies. Doctors manage medicines and their supply.
- **Letters and documents.** The team shares letters and documents with the patient; each one records when it was shared and by whom.
- **Forms.** Every form a patient sends is kept, as asked and answered, and cannot be changed.
- **Safeguarding alerts.** Read by the whole clinic team; only doctors add or close them.
- **Guardians.** A PA or admin links a guardian to a patient, and chooses whether they can only view or can also act.
- **Care team.** PAs, admins and doctors keep it up to date from the **Patient list**.
- **Registration, blood tests, call recordings and questions** each have their own part of the record.

## How it works

1. **Notes.** A note is a draft until its author chooses **Sign and lock**. The note records, for the CQC, that all available treatment options, customised options and NICE options, and side effects, were discussed, with the options offered. These are fixed once the note is signed.
2. **Draft notes from calls.** A recorded call arrives as a draft note in the clinician's name, to check and sign (see the recording guide).
3. **Letters.** Only doctors sign letters. A doctor's signature is stored privately and read only on the server.
4. **Sharing.** A document is visible to the patient only once it is shared. The share is recorded.
5. **Guardians.** A guardian sees the patient's portal. One who can only view sees no action buttons. A patient cannot remove a guardian: a PA or admin does it from the record.
6. **Safeguarding.** A doctor adds an alert with a type, a severity, a note and a review date. Doctors, nurses, health coaches, PAs and admins can read it. Patients and guardians never see it.

## How to try it

Use a fictional patient.

1. Open **Patient list** and choose a patient.
2. Add a note, save it as a draft, then choose **Sign and lock**. Try to edit the signed note: it cannot be changed.
3. Add an allergy and a medicine.
4. Upload a letter or document and share it. Sign in as the patient and open **Letters and documents**.
5. As a doctor, add a safeguarding alert. Sign in as a PA and check that it is nowhere to be seen.
6. As an admin, open **Activity** to see each of these steps in the log.

**What you should see:** a health coach and a doctor outside the care team can both open the record, and they read the safeguarding alerts too; only a doctor can add or close one.
