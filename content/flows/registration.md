## What it does

The clinic's registration form, recreated from the one sent through Semble. Every patient added from 19 November 2026 completes it once, for themselves, before they can use the portal. Patients who were already on the books, including those who come over from Semble, are not asked.

It collects:

- Title, address, gender and phone.
- Next of kin: name, relationship and telephone.
- GP: name, practice and address.
- Sex at birth and occupation.
- Four questions on why they are coming and their health goals.
- A photo ID (passport or driving licence; PDF, JPEG or PNG up to 10 MB).
- Their consent: that the details are accurate and they agree to the Privacy Notice and the Terms of Treatment and Fees, a choice not to receive news and insights from the doctors, and a consent to WhatsApp messages about their care.

Name, date of birth and email come from the invitation and are shown, not typed again.

## How it works

1. The patient accepts the invitation and signs in. The portal shows only the form until it is complete.
2. They answer every required question, add the photo ID and tick the agreement. The form cannot be sent without them.
3. On sending, the answers are kept three ways: as the registration on the record, as a permanent copy of the form in **Forms** (patient) and **Submitted forms** (team), and as a fill for any gap on the patient's record (address, phone, sex at birth, gender). What the record already holds is never overwritten.
4. Consenting to WhatsApp turns WhatsApp on for the four kinds of message.
5. The photo ID goes to a private store. Only PAs and admins can open it, and only through the clinic screen. Every opening is written to the activity log. A PA or admin marks the identity as checked once they have compared it with the patient.
6. A registration is permanent: nobody can change or remove it. The photo ID is kept for good until the clinic decides otherwise (an open question).

## How to try it

Use a fictional patient and an email address you can open.

1. Invite a new patient (see the invitations guide) and accept the invitation.
2. Fill in the form with made-up answers. For the photo ID, use any small image or PDF that is not a real document.
3. Send the form. You should reach the portal home.
4. Sign in as a PA or admin, open the patient's record and find the **Registration** section.
5. Open the photo ID, then mark the identity as checked.
6. Open the **Activity** screen (admins) to see the opening of the ID and the check.
7. In **Submitted forms** on the record, open the registration copy: the questions as asked and the answers.

**What you should see:** a doctor or a health coach on the care team can read the details but cannot open the photo ID, and the patient cannot read it back.
