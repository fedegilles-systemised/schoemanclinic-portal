## What it does

Fathom is replaced. From a patient's record, a doctor, nurse or health coach records a call. The recording is transcribed, and the transcript arrives on the record as a draft note in the clinician's name, to check and sign. Once signed, the note is final like any other.

The clinic already has patients' consent to record. Transcription is by AssemblyAI in the EU. It is switched on once the clinic has signed AssemblyAI's data processing agreement (see the Roadmap).

## How it works

1. On the patient's record, in the **Record a call** card, the clinician chooses **Start recording**. The browser asks to use the microphone and, for a video call, to share the screen or tab so that the call's own sound is recorded as well. Without screen sharing only the microphone is recorded.
2. They choose **Stop and save**. The audio goes to a private store in the clinic's own account.
3. The recording is sent for transcription. Its state shows as uploading, transcribing, transcribed or failed. A failed one can be checked again.
4. The transcript becomes a draft note in the clinician's name. They read it, correct it and choose **Sign and lock**.
5. A transcript too long for a note is shortened in the note, and the full audio is kept with the recording.
6. AssemblyAI's own copy is deleted once the transcript has been fetched. The clinic's copy of the audio stays in its private store. How long it stays is an open question.

## How to try it

It needs AssemblyAI's agreement signed first. Once it is on:

1. As a doctor, open a test patient's record and find the **Record a call** card.
2. Choose **Start recording**, allow the microphone, talk for a minute, and choose **Stop and save**.
3. Wait for the state to show transcribed.
4. Open the draft note, check it and sign it.

**What you should see:** the note is in your name, the audio is not readable by patients or guardians, and only a doctor, nurse or health coach can record.
